# Week 4: Pricing a two-sided platform

## Slide 1
The subsidy side and the money side. Platforms often charge one side little or nothing and earn their revenue from the other side.

## Slide 2
Choosing the subsidy side. Subsidise the side that is more price-sensitive and whose presence the other side values most. Charge the side that is less price-sensitive and gains more from reaching the other side.

## Slide 3
Discussion: a food-delivery platform charges restaurants a commission and gives diners free-delivery vouchers. Is it subsidising the right side? What would change your answer?
