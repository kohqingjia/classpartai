# Week 3: Platform competition and market tipping

## Slide 1
When does a platform market tip to a single winner, and when do several platforms survive?

## Slide 2
Tipping. With strong network effects an early lead can become self-reinforcing: new users join the bigger network, which makes it more attractive still. A market that runs this to completion has tipped.

## Slide 3
Three things that stop a market tipping: multi-homing (users can cheaply use several platforms at once), local network effects (value comes from nearby users only), and differentiation (users want different things).
