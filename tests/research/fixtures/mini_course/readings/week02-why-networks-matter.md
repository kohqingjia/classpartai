# Week 2 reading: Why networks matter

## Page 1
A product has a network effect when each user's benefit depends on how many other people use it. A telephone is the classic case: one telephone is useless, two can call each other, and every new subscriber adds possible calls for everyone already connected.

## Page 2
Network effects should not be confused with economies of scale. A large firm with economies of scale is cheaper to buy from; a large platform with network effects is more useful to belong to. A firm can enjoy one without the other: a steel mill has scale economies but no network effect, while a small but busy local marketplace can have strong network effects at modest scale.
